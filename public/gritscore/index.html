Build your scorecard

Upload a PDF resume or paste the text, then list the work you want scored. Proof is what moves the number: an openable link beats a screenshot, and a referee confirmation lifts the self-reported cap.

1. About you

Defaults to your Grit&Gigs profile name.

2. Resume (optional but recommended)

Up to 5 MB. The file is read and discarded unless you tick the box below.

3. Work items

Two to five strong items score better than ten thin ones. Describe the hard part and the result.

4. Sharing

The public page shows your score, tier, verification status, top skills and strongest proof. Visitors can leave a star rating and a short review. It is labelled a Proof Score — never a hiring verdict.
