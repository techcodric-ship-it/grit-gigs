Frequently Asked Questions

How much should a freelancer charge per hour in India?

It depends on your skill and experience. Beginners typically charge ₹150–₹500/hour, while experienced freelancers in web development, design, and specialised skills charge ₹800–₹3,000+/hour. Use the free Grit&Gigs rate calculator for a personalised estimate.

Is the Grit&Gigs rate calculator free?

Yes. It is 100% free — enter your skill, experience, and hours to get an instant on-screen estimate plus a full breakdown emailed to you. No credit card, no sign-up needed.

How do I calculate a freelance project budget in India?

Estimate the hours the project will take, then multiply by a realistic Indian market hourly rate for that skill. The Grit&Gigs rate calculator does this instantly for both freelancers and clients.
