How to Hire a Freelancer in India: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Define Your Project

Before you post anything, answer these:

  • What exactly do you need? (website, logo, app, content)
  • What's your budget? (₹5,000? ₹50,000? ₹5,00,000)
  • What's your deadline? (1 week? 1 month?)
  • What skills are required? (React, Photoshop, writing)

The more specific you are, the better bids you'll get.

Step 2: Post Your Project

On Grit&Gigs, posting a project is free:

  1. Go to gritandgigs.in/projects
  2. Click "Post a Project"
  3. Add title, description, budget, deadline
  4. Select category (Development, Design, Writing, etc.)
  5. Post it

You'll start receiving bids within hours.

Step 3: Review Bids & Profiles

Don't just look at price. Check:

  • Profile completeness — Does the freelancer have a photo, bio, and portfolio?
  • Reviews — What do other clients say?
  • Verification — Is the freelancer KYC verified?
  • Response time — Do they reply quickly?

Step 4: Start with a Small Test Project

Before committing ₹50,000, give a ₹5,000 test project. See their quality, communication, and deadlines. If they deliver well, hire them for bigger projects.

Step 5: Use Escrow Payments

Never pay upfront to a freelancer you don't know. Use escrow:

  1. Deposit project amount into escrow
  2. Freelancer starts work
  3. You review and approve
  4. Payment released to freelancer

This protects both you and the freelancer.

Red Flags to Watch Out For

When hiring freelancers (on any platform), avoid:

  • No profile photo — Legitimate freelancers have photos
  • No portfolio — Always ask for past work samples
  • Too cheap — If someone quotes ₹500 for a website, run
  • No contract — Always have scope, timeline, and payment terms in writing
  • Upfront payment — Use escrow, never pay 100% before work starts
