    How it works

    1. Search a company — choose the role you are applying for.
    2. Read the sourced report — official links, recent news, full provenance and an AI summary marked as an estimate.
    3. Practise aptitude and interviews — timed quizzes with explanations, mock sessions with category feedback.
    4. Contribute proof, unlock aggregates — approved pay or placement records release anonymised company figures for everyone.
