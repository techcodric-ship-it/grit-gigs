What is Escrow and How It Protects Freelancers in India

July 11, 2026 — 4 min read

If you've ever been ghosted by a client after delivering work, or paid a freelancer upfront only to receive poor quality — you know the pain. Escrow is the solution that protects both sides.

What is Escrow?

Escrow is a financial arrangement where a third party holds a payment until both sides of a transaction are fulfilled. In freelancing, the client deposits money into escrow before work starts. The freelancer sees the funds are secured and begins working. Once the work is delivered and approved, the payment is released.

Escrow protects freelancers from non-payment and protects clients from incomplete or substandard work. It's the most trusted payment model in online freelancing.

How Escrow Works on Grit&Gigs

Escrow here is enforced by the client's own Grit&Gigs wallet, and the money moves at bid-accept time — before any work starts. Here is the actual flow:

  • 1. Client funds their wallet — The client tops up via UPI, card, or net banking. Razorpay processes this top-up; it is the payment gateway, not the escrow account.
  • 2. Client accepts a bid — The bid amount is checked against the client's wallet balance. If it isn't there, the acceptance is rejected. If it is, the money is debited from the client's balance in the same operation that marks the bid as accepted.
  • 3. Work begins — The freelancer can see the agreed amount is already committed, so there is no exposure on the freelancer's side for the payment itself.
  • 4. Freelancer delivers — Work is submitted and the project moves to delivered.
  • 5. Client releases payment — The freelancer is credited (minus platform commission) only after delivery, and the release is blocked if the project has not been delivered.
  • 6. If a project is cancelled — Any amount held is returned to the client's wallet automatically, with a transaction record explaining it.

Because the deduction and the acceptance happen together atomically, a client cannot accept several bids they could never collectively pay for. Each acceptance must be funded at the moment it happens.

What Escrow Does Not Solve

Being direct about the limits: escrow protects the payment, not the files. Once final source files, logos, or a completed website are in a client's hands, nothing technical prevents reuse, resale, or re-uploading. Watermarked, domain-locked previews released as clean originals only when payment clears is the fix — it is not built yet. If your work is easy to copy, escrow is necessary but not sufficient on its own.

Why Indian Freelancers Need Escrow

Indian freelancers face unique challenges. Many clients are small businesses or individuals who prefer paying after work is done — which leaves freelancers vulnerable. Up to 30% of Indian freelancers report being ghosted without payment at least once.

Escrow eliminates the payment risk. Because the amount leaves the client's wallet when the bid is accepted, the freelancer starts work knowing the money is already committed — and the client cannot accept work they have not funded. It builds trust in a market where trust is often the biggest barrier.

Escrow vs Other Payment Methods

  • Bank transfer / UPI — Once sent, you can't get the money back. No protection for either party.
  • Advance payment — Client takes all the risk. If the freelancer disappears, the client loses everything.
  • Post-delivery payment — Freelancer takes all the risk. Client can ghost after receiving work.
  • "We'll hold it for you" — A record claiming funds are in escrow, with no rule about when money can leave. This is the most common version and it protects almost nobody.
  • Funded escrow (Grit&Gigs) — The client's balance is debited at acceptance, so the money is already committed before work starts. The freelancer is credited on delivery release. The client cannot accept work they haven't paid for.

The distinction that matters: between a promise that funds are held and money that has actually left an account the client controls. Ask any platform which one they mean.

Is Escrow Safe?

Wallet funding is processed by Razorpay, one of India's largest and most trusted payment gateways, so top-ups are encrypted and settled through standard rails. The escrow itself is arithmetic on your own wallet balance, not a third-party custody account: the amount is debited at bid acceptance and credited to the freelancer on delivery release. Because the deduction is conditional on the balance still being sufficient at that instant, bids accepted in parallel cannot overspend the same funds.

Commission, Not Escrow Fees

There is no fee for holding funds in escrow. Commission is charged on the payout when a project is released, and it comes out of the freelancer's share, not on top of the client's agreed amount. Barter trades are free. Withdrawals from your wallet are processed via UPI or bank transfer, and the minimum withdrawal threshold is ₹100. There are no hidden charges on the escrow itself.
