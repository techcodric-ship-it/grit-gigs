FAQ

Hiring a video editor in India — your questions answered

How much does it cost to hire a video editor in India?

Video editor services on Grit & Gigs start from ₹2,000. Most freelancers offer three packages (Basic, Standard, Premium) so you can pick a price and scope that fits your budget. There is no platform fee to post a project and you only pay when you choose a freelancer.

How fast will I get my video editing work done?

Most freelancers deliver first concepts within 2–4 days. Express delivery options are available for a faster turnaround when you need things urgently.

What if I don't like the results?

Every package includes revisions, and many freelancers offer unlimited revisions until you are happy. Your payment is held in secure escrow and is only released after you approve the final delivery.

Will I own the work and get all the files?

Yes. On final delivery you receive full usage rights along with the final video files and, on request, the editable project files, so you can use the work anywhere your business needs it.

How do payments work and is my money safe?

Payments are held in secure escrow. The freelancer does not receive your money until you approve the delivered work, which protects you against poor results. Revisions and clear deliverables are guaranteed before any release of funds.

Can I talk to the freelancer in Hindi or Hinglish?

Absolutely. All freelancers on Grit & Gigs are Indian and can communicate in English, Hindi, Hinglish or their regional language. This makes explaining your requirements much easier.
