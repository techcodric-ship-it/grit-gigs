Spotlight: get your gig or project in front of the right people

Your gig is only seen by people who happen to scroll past it. Spotlight puts it at the top of the feed for people who are actively searching for what you offer — and shows you exactly how many people it reached.

What it actually does

When you buy a boost, you pick up to five keywords. Anyone whose recent searches match those keywords sees your gig or project at the top of their feed, marked ✦ Boosted. When they open it, that counts as a click.

You see three numbers the whole time it runs: reached (distinct people who saw it), opened (how many clicked), and the percentage who opened. If nobody searched your keywords, the numbers stay at zero — we will not dress that up.

What we will not promise you

  • No guaranteed view count. Reach depends on how many people are searching your keywords that week. If there are none, a boost reaches no one.
  • No guaranteed leads or hires. We sell visibility, not customers. Only you can decide who to work with.
  • No invented urgency. We will not tell you "12 people are viewing this right now" unless that is literally true.

Before you pay, the boost screen shows how many real searches matched your keywords in the last 7 days. That is the honest ceiling of who a boost can reach. Check it first.
