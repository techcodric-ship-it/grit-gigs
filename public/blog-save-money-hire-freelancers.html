  • Agency 1: ₹3,50,000

Then I hired freelancers on Grit&Gigs. Same quality. Same deadlines. Total cost: ₹47,000.

Why Agencies Charge So Much

Agencies aren't scamming you. They have real costs:

  • Office rent — Mumbai/Bangalore office = ₹3-5 lakh/month
  • Sales team — 2-3 people just to get clients
  • Management layer — project managers, account managers, creative directors
  • Overhead — laptops, software, insurance, HR

When you pay ₹80,000 for a website, maybe ₹25,000 goes to the actual developer. The rest funds the agency's overhead.

When you hire a freelancer directly on Grit&Gigs, 90% goes to the person doing the work. That's why they can charge less and still earn more.

But Is the Quality the Same?

"But agencies have teams of experts! Freelancers are just random people!"

Here's what I learned:

  • Most agency "teams" are 1-2 senior people + junior freelancers. The senior person sells the project. The junior person does the work. You're already getting freelance quality — you're just paying the agency markup.
  • Freelancers on Grit&Gigs are verified. KYC-verified, reviews from real clients, portfolio samples visible. You can see their work before hiring.
  • Escrow protection. Your money is held safely until you approve the work. If they don't deliver, you get a refund.

The logo designer I hired had 4.9 stars and 142 reviews. She was better than the agency's "creative director" who handed my project to an intern.

How to Hire Freelancers the Smart Way

Here's the 5-step system I use now:

1. Post Your Project (2 minutes)

Go to Grit&Gigs, describe what you need, set your budget. Freelancers come to you with proposals. You don't chase anyone.

2. Compare Portfolios (10 minutes)

Look at their past work, reviews, and ratings. Shortlist 2-3 freelancers. Don't just pick the cheapest — pick the one whose style matches your vision.

3. Start with a Small Test (₹500-2,000)

Before committing ₹15,000 for a website, pay ₹1,000 for a landing page. If they deliver well, give them the bigger project. If not, you've lost ₹1,000 — not ₹80,000.

4. Use Escrow (Always)

Never pay outside the platform. Escrow protects both you and the freelancer. Payment is released only when you approve the work.

5. Build Relationships

Once you find a good freelancer, stick with them. My logo designer now handles all my design work. She knows my brand, my style, my preferences. No agency can match that.

When to Use an Agency Instead

I'm not anti-agency. Here's when agencies make sense:

  • You need a 50-page enterprise website with custom integrations, databases, and ongoing maintenance — agencies have the bandwidth
  • You need a full rebrand with market research, focus groups, and brand guidelines — that's a team effort
  • You have ₹5+ lakhs to spend and don't want to manage individual freelancers

But for 90% of small business needs — logo, website, social media, content, video — freelancers are the better choice.
