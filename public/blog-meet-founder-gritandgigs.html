Meet the Founder Behind Grit&Gigs

Why I built India's first freelance & skill barter platform — and my mission to empower 1 million Indian freelancers.

Published July 28, 2026 · 5 min read

Who Am I?

I'm Amuthavanan E, the founder of Grit&Gigs.

I'm a solo developer, a dreamer, and someone who believes Indian talent deserves better.

I built Grit&Gigs alone — every line of code, every design, every email. No team. No funding. Just late nights and a relentless belief that India needs its own freelance platform.

Why I Built Grit&Gigs

It started with a simple observation:

Indian freelancers are some of the most talented people in the world. They build software for Google, design for Apple, write for Fortune 500 companies. But when they work through global platforms, they lose 20% of their income to fees.

A freelancer earning ₹5,00,000 a year on Upwork loses ₹1,00,000 to platform fees. That's a month's rent. That's their child's school fees. That's a family's medical emergency fund.

I asked myself: Why is Indian money flowing to American platforms when we have everything we need right here?

That question kept me awake at night.

So I built Grit&Gigs.

What Grit&Gigs Stands For

The name says it all:

Grit — The perseverance to keep going when things get hard. Every freelancer knows this.

Gigs — The work that pays the bills and builds careers.

Grit&Gigs is built on three pillars:

  • Freelancing — Connect with clients at 5% fees (not 20%)
  • Skill Barter — Trade skills without spending money
  • MicroEquity — Invest in local businesses (coming soon)

No hidden fees. No USD conversion charges. No 20% cuts.

Just Indian talent, Indian prices, and an Indian platform.

The Journey So Far

  • 200+ freelancers have joined the platform
  • Users from across India — Chennai, VIT Bhopal, Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore
  • Freelancers in design, development, writing, marketing, and more
  • Barter matches happening — skills being traded without money

But we're still early. Very early.

I won't pretend we have millions of users. We don't. What we have is a growing community of people who believe in this mission.

And I'm personally talking to every user. Every message. Every feedback. Because this platform is built for you.

What I Promise You

To freelancers:

  • You will never pay more than 5% fees
  • Your payments will be protected by escrow
  • Your skills will be valued — not exploited

To clients:

  • You will find verified, KYC-completed talent
  • You will pay in INR, not USD
  • You will get quality work — or your money back

To everyone:

  • I will answer your messages personally
  • I will listen to your feedback
  • I will keep building until Grit&Gigs is the #1 platform for Indian talent

Why Trust Me?

I'm not a funded startup. I don't have a marketing team. I don't run ads.

What I have is:

  • A deep love for building things that matter
  • A belief that Indian talent deserves better
  • The grit to keep going
