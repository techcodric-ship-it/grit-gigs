How Squads Actually Earn on Grit&Gigs

Published September 6, 2026 • 5 min read

One freelancer wins projects that fit one freelancer. A Grit Circle — up to 6 freelancers on the Squad plan — wins projects that fit a whole team, and that's where the income jumps.

Here's the complete money flow of a squad: where the income comes from, how payouts get split, and the numbers that make it worth it.

Where Squad Income Comes From

Squads earn through exactly two routes, both run through the platform:

1. Squad Services

Your circle publishes a squad service — one combined offer covering the skills of every member, priced once and delivered to one deadline. When a client orders it, the whole circle works on it and the whole circle gets paid.

2. Squad Projects

Clients also post SQUAD projects — jobs deliberately too big for a solo freelancer, like "design + build + launch content for our product". Your circle bids as one team and wins the whole thing.

Entry rule: to bid on SQUAD projects, a Grit Circle needs at least 4 active members. That's the unlock for the biggest squad payouts.

The Money Flow, Step by Step

  1. Client pays into escrow. Whether they order a squad service or accept a squad bid, the full amount is held safely the same way all Grit&Gigs payments are.
  2. The circle delivers. Every member works their part — design, code, content, SEO, whatever the brief needs.
  3. Client approves. Money is released from escrow to the squad.
  4. The payout splits equally across the members who delivered, and each member is charged their own plan's commission.

No chasing each other for money. No "I did more than you". The platform handles the split, and the review lands on the circle's shared reputation.

The Math: Why Teams Earn More

Two levers make squad income bigger than solo income — larger deal sizes and lower commission.

Example — a ₹1,20,000 project won by a 4-member circle on the Squad plan:

Line item Amount
Project payout ₹1,20,000
Split equally across 4 members ₹30,000 each
Squad plan commission (1%) ₹1,200 total
Each member keeps (before their own plan commission) ₹29,700

Compare that to the same project handled solo on Starter at 10% — a team wins briefs no solo freelancer can even bid on, at one-tenth the commission.

The savings alone often pay for the Squad plan — and that's before the bigger deal sizes.

A Circle in Action

Meet Pixel Forge: a founder plus a designer, a backend engineer, and a content & SEO writer. Five members, Squad plan.

A client posts a SQUAD project: "Build our SaaS landing page + billing flow + launch content", budget ₹1,20,000, 14-day deadline. No solo freelancer can honestly bid for that brief. Pixel Forge does — one team, one quote, one delivery date.

The designer owns the interface, the engineer builds the billing flow, the writer produces the launch content. The client deals with one circle. The payout is split by the platform, and a 5-star review lands on the circle's shared reputation — which makes the next, even bigger project that much easier to win.

Income That Compounds

Solo reputation compounds review by review. A circle's reputation compounds review by bigger review — because every win is larger, and every client adds to the team profile that clients see before they hire.

The three levers, in short:

  • Bigger deals — multi-skill SQUAD projects a solo freelancer can't bid on
  • Lower fees — 1% commission on the Squad plan
  • Shared reputation — the circle's rating grows with every delivered win
