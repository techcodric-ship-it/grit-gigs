Why Freelance Platforms Fail Indian Business Owners

The big platforms — Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com — were built for American clients hiring American freelancers. They don't understand India:

1. No Real KYC Verification

Anyone can create a profile with a fake name and stock photo. Upwork doesn't verify identity for Indian freelancers. Neither does Fiverr. A "Top Rated" badge just means they completed projects — it doesn't mean they're a real person.

2. They Encourage You to Pay Outside

Upwork charges 20% commission. So freelancers naturally say: "Pay me directly, you'll save 20%." And you do. Because ₹24,000 is a lot of money. The moment you pay outside, the platform washes its hands off. You have zero protection.

3. No Escrow for Indian Bank Transfers

Upwork's escrow only works if you pay through their system in USD. If you're paying an Indian freelancer in INR via bank transfer, you have no escrow protection. The money goes directly to them.

4. Indian Police Won't Help

Cyber crime cells are overloaded. A ₹85,000 freelance dispute is not their priority. Even if you file an FIR, the freelancer used a fake bank account. Your money is gone.

How to Hire Freelancers in India Safely — 5 Rules I Follow Now

After losing ₹85,000, I developed a system. I've hired 12 freelancers since then. Paid over ₹8 lakhs. Zero issues.

Rule #1: Never Pay Outside the Platform

This is non-negotiable. If a freelancer asks you to pay "directly to save fees," they are either:

  • A scammer planning to disappear
  • Someone who doesn't understand why platforms exist

Either way, don't hire them. The platform fee is insurance. ₹6,000 on a ₹30,000 project is worth it for the peace of mind.

Better yet: Use a platform that charges 0% commission on certain plans. That removes the incentive to go outside entirely.

Rule #2: Only Hire KYC-Verified Freelancers

KYC means the freelancer has submitted government ID — Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport. A real person with a real identity. If they scam you, they can be traced.

On most platforms, KYC is optional. On Grit&Gigs, we made it mandatory for every freelancer who wants the verified badge.

Rule #3: Always Use Escrow for Milestone Payments

Here's the safest payment structure:

  • Milestone 1 (30%): After project scope is signed
  • Milestone 2 (30%): After first deliverable is approved
  • Milestone 3 (30%): After final deliverable is approved
  • Milestone 4 (10%): After 30-day warranty period

Each milestone is held in escrow. The freelancer gets paid only after you approve the work. If they disappear, the money comes back to you.

Rule #4: Do a ₹5,000 Test First

Before committing to a ₹1,00,000 project, give the freelancer a ₹5,000 test task. Same skill, smaller scope.

  • Need a website? Ask for one landing page first.
  • Need a logo? Ask for 3 concepts first.
  • Need content? Ask for one sample article first.

If they deliver quality work on time for the test, they'll deliver for the full project. If they fail, you lost ₹5,000 instead of ₹1,00,000.

Rule #5: Check Reddit and Twitter Before Hiring

Before hiring a freelancer, search their name or username on:

  • Reddit: r/IndiaFreelancing, r/Upwork — clients post warnings
  • Twitter/X: Search "@username scam" — you'll find if others were cheated
  • LinkedIn: Look for mutual connections who can vouch

This takes 5 minutes. It saved me from 3 scammers in the past 6 months.
